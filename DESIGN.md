# Design

This repo holds three films. The one submitted for the assignment is `films/the-last-buna`, and Part 1 covers it. Part 2 is the design record of the first film made with the system, `films/gebisa-ejeta`, which is where most of the pipeline's lessons came from.

# Part 1: The Last Buna (the submitted film)

The audience and feeling below were written into `films/the-last-buna/brief.json` on 26 November 2026, before the film's first model call (the first `the-last-buna` line in `runs.jsonl`, 11:45 UTC).

## The story

A made-up story, set in Jimma. Emaye Zewditu makes coffee every morning. Coffee is poured three times, and the third and last cup is called baraka, blessing. Her son Dawit left for Addis Ababa seven years ago, and she told him the third cup would always be his. Every morning she pours it and it goes cold. One rainy morning the blue door opens, and he pours the third cup for her.

The people are invented. The full text, in the director's telling, is the `story` field of the brief, and it says in its first line that it is made up.

## Audience

Ethiopians, especially young adults living away from their families, and viewers abroad who are curious about Ethiopian life. Watched on a phone, on YouTube, Reels or TikTok.

## Intent

The feeling at the end: love and longing for a parent. However far you go, do not forget the person who is waiting for you at home.

## What this decides

| Because of the audience and intent | The film will |
|---|---|
| They are Ethiopians away from home | be narrated in Amharic, with English subtitles for viewers abroad |
| They watch on a phone | be designed for 9:16 first, with every subject in the centre so the 16:9 export also works |
| They scroll | use 12 short shots of 4.5 to 7 seconds, not fewer long ones |
| The feeling is longing | spend its first half on the daily ceremony, in close shots of hands, beans, the jebena and cups, so the missing person is felt before he is named |
| The feeling must land at the end | turn on one object, the third cup, and end on it being handed to her |
| The viewer should see their own mother | never show a face: only hands, backs and a silhouette |

## How people are shown

Nobody in the story is real, but the system cannot know that about a story the director tells, so it applies the rule for real people: no recognisable face. The storyboard's `world_block` says any person is seen only from behind, from far away, or as hands. The first set of pictures showed the mother's face in three shots; those prompts were rewritten and the pictures remade (the change is in the git history of `films/the-last-buna/storyboard.json`, 26 November).

## The voice

The narrator is a stock Amharic text-to-speech voice, `am-yohanes-calm`, from Addis AI. This is a paid service and is not in the assignment's free stack. It was used because Gemini's free text-to-speech quota was exhausted (FAILURES.md, F7) and because it was compared with Gemini on the Abebe Bikila film. The 12 lines cost 0.03 USD by the run log. The committed audio is reused, so rebuilding the film needs no key and makes no call.

## The score

Three candidates were generated by ACE-Step 1.5 from the storyboard's `music` block, with seeds 1, 2 and 3.

| | |
|---|---|
| In the film | Candidate 1 |
| Why this one | Not yet a choice by ear. It was put in as the first of the three so the cut has real music in place of the placeholder chord. The director's choice and reason go here. |

## What was generated, and what was not

Nine of the twelve shots are video generated by Wan 2.2 from the shot's picture, in both shapes. Shots s03, s08 and s12 are a camera move over the picture: in the generated clip for s03 the hand changed skin tone, in the wide clip for s08 the hand took the cups away where the line says she sets them down, and in s12 the cups multiplied.

# Part 2: Gebisa Ejeta (the first film)

Written on 24 November 2026, before any image, audio, video or script was generated.
The same audience and intent are in `films/gebisa-ejeta/brief.json`, which every agent reads.

## The story

Gebisa Ejeta: how a boy from a village in Ethiopia became the plant scientist who bred sorghum that survives both drought and the parasitic weed striga.

## Audience

Ethiopian students and young graduates aged 16–25, watching on a phone on TikTok or Telegram.

## Intent

The feeling at the end: pride and possibility. Someone who started where I started changed how a continent eats.

## What this decides

| Because of the audience and intent | The film will |
|---|---|
| They watch on a phone | be designed for 9:16 first; the 16:9 export is a crop of the same shots |
| They often watch with the sound off | carry the whole story in the subtitles as well as the voice |
| They scroll | open on a picture that raises a question within the first three seconds |
| The feeling is "someone who started where I started" | spend its opening shots on the village and the walk to school, not on awards |
| The feeling is possibility, not biography | end on what the work made possible, not on a list of prizes |

## How the subject is shown

Gebisa Ejeta is a living person. The film never shows his face or likeness, never imitates his voice, and gives him no invented words.
The narrator speaks about him in the third person. The pictures show places, fields, plants, hands and seed.
The record of this decision and the sources for every fact are in `ETHICS.md`.

## The score

Three candidates were generated by ACE-Step 1.5 from one brief (the `music` block of the storyboard), with seeds 1, 2 and 3. Each was also mixed under the narration, so it could be judged as it sits in the film.
